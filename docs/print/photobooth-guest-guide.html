Photobooth Guide

Welcome to the photobooth!
It's self-serve and super easy.
Just follow these 3 steps:
👉1. Tap to Start
😄2. Smile!
📲3. Get Your Photo
Have fun — take as many photos as you like! 🎉
STEP 1

Start & Choose Your Photo

1
From the main screen, tap "Start Photobooth."
👆 Tap the big pink button at the bottom to take your photo. That's it!
🖼️ Optional: tap a layout at the top like 2x2 or Strip for a fun multi-photo frame — or leave it on Single for one photo.
STEP 2

Get Ready & Smile!

2
After you tap the button, a countdown appears. Look at the camera and strike a pose!
⏱️ You'll see 3 … 2 … 1 … then the screen flashes — that's your photo being taken!
🖼️ If you picked a multi-photo layout, it repeats the countdown for each shot automatically. Just keep smiling!
STEP 3

Get Your Photo

3
Once your photo is ready, send it straight to your phone!
📱 Scan the QR code shown on screen with your phone's camera to download your photo instantly (if the host has upload enabled).
💬 Or type your phone number and tap Send SMS — or your email and tap Send Email.
🖨️ Tap Print to print a copy right there at the booth (if a printer is connected)!

A Few Friendly Tips

  • 📸 Take as many photos as you'd like — the more, the merrier!
  • 👯 Grab your friends and family for group shots.
  • 🤳 There's also a small Bluetooth remote at the booth — see the little card next to it for how to use it hands-free.
  • 🙋 Need help? Ask a member of the event staff.
  • 💖 Thanks for stopping by the photobooth — enjoy!
